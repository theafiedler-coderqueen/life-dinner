In short: Life Dinner collects no data. No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores – only on your device

  • your first names
  • the dates of your Life Dinners
  • your goals and how you rated them
  • the photos of your evenings
  • settings such as language and reminder

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. The app has no server and does not connect to any analytics or advertising service.

Permissions

  • Camera: only for the photo of your evening. It is stored in the app.
  • Photos (add only): if you also want to save that photo to your photo library. When you pick a photo from your library, the iOS picker is used – the app only sees the photo you choose.
  • Notifications: one reminder the day before your Life Dinner, scheduled locally on your iPhone.
  • Calendar: “Add to calendar” opens the iOS calendar dialog, where you decide whether to save the event. “Share” sends a calendar file through the iOS share sheet, using the app you choose there.

Backups and export

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, iOS includes the app’s data in your iPhone backup. You control this in the iOS settings; I have no access to it. “Export memories” in the settings hands your memories to the iOS share sheet – only when you tap it, and only to the destination you choose.

Deleting your data

When you delete the app, all of its data is removed from your iPhone.

Your rights

As I receive no personal data from the app, there is nothing I could disclose, correct or delete. If you have questions, just write to me. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

When you e-mail me

If you contact me, I use your e-mail address and message only to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them once your request is settled.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you open it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The website sets no cookies and loads no external fonts or scripts.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your data, I will update this policy and the date above.
